A 3D-printed enclosure designer for maker electronics: drag & drop
connectors, standoffs, compartments and vents on a box, generate
print-ready STLs.

## The enclosure in one paragraph

A modern parametric box: continuous outer shell with large corner
radii and filleted top edges, flush snap-fit lid (no visible screws,
opened through two discreet side notches), optional screwed lid.
Sizes S (70×50×30) to XL (180×130×60) plus free custom inner
dimensions. Everything prints without supports.
